# LifeMate — Feature Priorities v1

## MUST — first useful product
- Independent accounts, verified email, sign-in/out, forgot/reset/change password.
- Profile/life-context model; Teen, Parent/Guardian, Adult Member, Family Admin relationships.
- Dynamic Family Workspace, invitations, membership changes and server-enforced permissions.
- Today view, Calendar, Tasks, routines/goals baseline, reminders and rescheduling.
- School year/term, subjects, class schedule, assignments, exams and study sessions.
- Controlled sharing and family calendar.
- Parent dashboard for authorized schedule, academic workload/progress and support summaries.
- Persian RTL, responsive web/PWA, Android installable client, app-like iPhone Home Screen experience.
- Privacy controls, audit events for sensitive policy/access changes, safety boundary.

## SHOULD — after core reliability
- Grades/progress trends and revision plans.
- Focus/study timer and workload balancing.
- AI Life Planner and Study Coach/Tutor.
- Teen wellbeing check-ins and Parent Coach summaries under explicit policy.
- Voice input/output after Persian quality/privacy validation.
- Offline cache for essential upcoming plan data and safe queued edits where conflict semantics are defined.

## LATER
- Rich gamification, streak economy and collectibles.
- LMS/school-system integrations.
- Social/community features.
- Native/App-Store iOS packaging when distribution conditions justify it.
- Marketplace/plugin ecosystem.
- Advanced household finance/work modules.

## V1 outcome
A family can securely join one workspace; each member can plan independently; a teen can manage a real school week; parents can see the authorized information needed to support the teen; shared family items work; reminders are useful; the product remains usable without AI.

## Non-goals
LifeMate V1 is not a surveillance product, clinical diagnosis/treatment system, location tracker, social network, school ERP, or autonomous decision-maker.
